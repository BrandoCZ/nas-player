Releases

What each released version of NAS Player brought. Every version includes everything from the versions before it.

Version 3.0

October 21, 2026

Downloads and storage

  • Download only on Wi-Fi or only while charging, each with a “Download anyway” override; targeted downloads ask first
  • Download your whole library to the iPhone at once, with a prompt about size, free space and limit; favorites first, then recently played, then the rest, with overall progress and pause
  • Download history with a queue and “Try again”
  • Depending on your connection, 1 song at a time on cellular, 2 on Wi-Fi away from home, 3 on your home network
  • Usage overview for Wi-Fi and cellular, with a notice above 1 GB per month
  • Check and repair downloaded files; clean up storage with multi-select; covers stored as small thumbnails

Listening

  • Sleep timer in the full-screen player: 15, 30 or 60 minutes, end of the song or the album, with a gentle fade-out
  • Your own playlists, “Save as playlist” for the up-next queue, and cover headers for all playlists
  • New album page with a cover that shrinks as you scroll and the title in the bar; zoom transition from the artist page; lock-screen cover changes instantly with each song
  • Statistics and a year in review (listening time, plays, top lists, habits, swipeable cards), with a local reminder on January 1 and a banner in January. Everything stays on your iPhone.

CarPlay and connection

  • If the connection to the NAS drops, playback pauses and shows why (connection lost, NAS not responding, playback paused) on the iPhone, in CarPlay and on the lock screen, then resumes by itself when the connection is back
  • Calmer, flicker-free shuffle, repeat and favorite buttons in Now Playing
  • Albums you remove disappear immediately; clearer error messages and a “Connection problems” section in the in-app help

Version 2.0

October 2026

CarPlay

  • Tabs for Home (recently played and favorites), Library (albums, artists, genres, shuffle all, offline), Playlists and Settings
  • Album pages with cover, name, artist and song count; covers appear instantly
  • Offline notice “No connection to the NAS”, with NAS-only songs greyed out
  • Now Playing with shuffle, repeat and favorite buttons, plus “Up Next”

Siri and Shortcuts

  • “Play the album … with NAS Player”, play an artist shuffled, “Play my favorites”, “Shuffle my music”, “Shuffle my downloaded music”, “Play my most played songs”
  • Several phrasings (“with”, “in” or “on” NAS Player); offline, only downloaded songs play. All commands

Library

  • Genres, with a cover mix per genre, and multi-disc albums with “CD 1”, “CD 2” headings
  • “Play” and “Shuffle” for whatever a list currently shows (All, Offline or Favorites)
  • Letter index along the edge of the Songs and Artists lists
  • “Search for NAS on the network” finds your NAS on the home network and fills in the address
  • The address field is pre-filled with “https://”

Design

  • The app speaks English, Czech, Spanish, Italian and French as well as German, including CarPlay and Siri
  • Album page in the color of the cover, with a zoom transition from the tile and back
  • Full-screen player grows out of the mini player, with a background in the cover color that changes softly with each song and no white flash
  • Transparent mini player; shuffle, favorite and repeat show their state like in Apple’s Music app

Version 1.0

October 13, 2026

  • Whole library with albums, artists and songs, search and covers
  • Streaming from your Synology NAS (Audio Station), saving songs as you stream, and downloading albums and artists on purpose for offline use, with a storage limit (20 GB by default)
  • Clear symbols for “on iPhone”, “partly” and “only on the NAS”
  • Favorites for songs, albums and artists; up next with shuffle and repeat; resume after a restart
  • Playback controls on the lock screen and in Control Center
  • Sign-in over https only, password in the iPhone keychain
  • Demo mode with sample music, no NAS needed
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts
