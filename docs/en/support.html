Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my NAS?

Open Settings (gear icon) in the app and tap “Search for NAS on the network” (same Wi-Fi, no VPN), or enter the address (with https), user name, and password. The NAS must be reachable with a valid certificate. The user needs access to Audio Station.

Can I try the app without a NAS?

Yes, with “Try the demo” on the start screen or in Settings. You hear sample music that is part of the app.

Where is my music on the iPhone and how much space does it use?

Downloaded songs are in the app's storage on the iPhone, not in the backup. Under “Storage” in Settings you see the space used and set a limit.

Why are some songs greyed out?

Songs that are only on the NAS are greyed out without a network. Downloaded songs (checkmark) also play offline.

Does it work in the car?

Yes, with CarPlay: Home, library, playlists, and settings. “Hey Siri, play my favorites with NAS Player” works as well. See all Siri commands.

The help in the app

The in-app help explains all symbols and gestures: tap the logo at the top left → “About NAS Player” → “Symbols and Controls”.
