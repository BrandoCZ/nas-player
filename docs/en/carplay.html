Your NAS music in CarPlay

NAS Player puts the music on your Synology NAS on the CarPlay display. You pick albums, playlists, and genres right in the car.

What you see in the car

  • Home: recently played albums and favorites as tiles
  • Library: albums with covers, plus playlists and genres
  • Now Playing: playback with controls
  • Siri: “Play my favorites with NAS Player” (all commands)

Set up

  1. Set up your NAS in the app (guide). The iPhone must have been connected to your NAS once.
  2. Connect the iPhone to CarPlay, wirelessly or with a cable.
  3. If NAS Player does not appear on the CarPlay display: on the iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay, choose your car, tap “Customize”, and add NAS Player. The names can differ depending on the iOS version.

On the road and offline

On the road, the iPhone needs a connection to your NAS, for example through a DDNS address. Albums you download to the iPhone before the trip also play without a network. Without a network, the app clearly shows in the car what can be played right now; the rest is grayed out.

Requirements

A Synology NAS with Audio Station, an iPhone with the app (NAS Player on the App Store), and a car or stereo with CarPlay. Without a NAS you can try the app in demo mode.
