Siri commands

NAS Player works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. The commands below work in English (the language of your iPhone and of Siri must be English). The same commands exist in the other languages of the app: German, Spanish, Italian, and French. Use the language switch at the top.

Before you start

  1. Open NAS Player once and wait until your song list has loaded.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap NAS Player under App Shortcuts (or open NAS Player → Settings → “Siri and Shortcuts” and tap the Shortcuts button), and make sure the Siri switch is on. Apple lets only you switch this on; an app cannot do it for you.
  3. Say “Hey Siri” followed by one of the commands below.

Commands

Play an album

Plays the album from the first song.

Replace the placeholder with an album from your library, for example “Greatest Hits”.

  • “Hey Siri, play the album <album> with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play <album> with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play the album <album> in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play <album> in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play <album> on NAS Player”

Shuffle an artist

Plays songs by the artist in random order.

Replace the placeholder with an artist from your library, for example “Avicii”.

  • “Hey Siri, play <artist> with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle <artist> with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play <artist> in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play <artist> on NAS Player”

Shuffle favorites

Plays your favorites (songs you marked with the heart) in random order.

  • “Hey Siri, play my favorites with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my favorites with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my favorites in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my favorites on NAS Player”

Shuffle downloaded music

Plays all songs that are downloaded to the iPhone in random order.

  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my downloaded music with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my downloaded music with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my downloaded music in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my downloaded music on NAS Player”

Shuffle everything

Plays your whole library in random order.

  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my music with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play random music with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my music in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, shuffle my music on NAS Player”

Most played songs

Plays the songs you have played most often.

  • “Hey Siri, play my most played songs with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my popular songs with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my top songs with NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my most played songs in NAS Player”
  • “Hey Siri, play my most played songs on NAS Player”

Tips

  • Say the name of the album or artist as it is shown in the app. If Siri understands a different name, tap the result and choose the right one.
  • Without a network, only songs that are downloaded to the iPhone are played.
  • In the Shortcuts app you can also run the commands with a tap or add them to the Home Screen and to the Action button.
  • Playing a single song by voice is not available yet.
  • If Siri answers “NAS Player hasn't added support for that”, you used a different sentence. Use one of the exact patterns above (with with, in, or on before NAS Player).
