Coming next

A rough look at what NAS Player may get in future versions. These are plans and ideas, not promises, and the order is not fixed. See Releases for what is already in the app.

Smarter library

  • Keeping your library and favorites in sync with the NAS automatically
  • Downloading the music you listen to most, without you having to ask
  • Faster, simpler connection when you are at home

Better sound and lyrics

  • More control over how your music sounds and plays
  • Song lyrics

More ways to listen

  • Widgets and more natural Siri voice commands
  • Audiobooks and podcasts
  • More audio formats

More devices

  • iPad
  • Apple Watch
  • Mac

More flexibility

  • Several NAS connections and users
  • A tool to find and fix inconsistencies in your music collection

Your ideas

Missing something? Tell us through GitHub Issues.
